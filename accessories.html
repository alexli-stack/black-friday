How do I check compatibility?

Start with your machine family, then confirm the exact model, generation, laser source and connector on the product page. For a replacement part, have your serial number ready when contacting support.

Does every laser need a water chiller?

Cooling requirements depend on the laser source and configuration. Glass-tube CO₂ systems and RF systems have different needs. Check your machine documentation before selecting a chiller.

What do I need for the XRF conveyor feeder?

The collection lists the Base Boost riser base as a requirement. Check the feeder product page for the complete setup and current compatibility.

Which replacement filter should I buy?

Match the exact FiltraBox unit, generation and filter stage. A machine-family match alone does not identify the correct replacement filter.
