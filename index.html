FAQ

Questions before you checkout

Which products are in the promotion?

The machines, accessories, and gift cards on this page. Open a product for the full description, current price, and inventory.

When does each week change?

The schedule lists the dates. The week that contains today is open. After the end date, that tab shows the closed message.

Are the prices on this page final?

Cards show the current store price. If a product has a compare-at price, the previous price sits beside it. Checkout charges the price on the product.

Can I combine offers?

Plan on one promotional discount per order.

Do you offer financing?

If a financing app is enabled, it appears at checkout. Approval, rates, and terms come from that provider.

How do I choose a machine?

Use the comparison: XRF for a desktop RF bed, Cobra for workshop glass-tube cutting, Hydra Gen 2 for industrial cabinets, VertiGo for drinkware.

Can these CO2 machines cut bare metal?

Bare metal is not listed as a cut material. Check the product page before you buy for a metal job.
